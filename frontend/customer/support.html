Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common customer support questions.

How do I view my account balance?

Open the My Accounts page from the customer dashboard to view your available account information.

How can I make a transfer?

Open Transfers from the customer navigation and follow the transfer instructions displayed there.

Where can I view my transactions?

Your recent account activity is available through the relevant account and transaction sections of the dashboard.

How do I manage my cards?

Open the Cards section to review available card information and supported card-management options.

What information should I include in a support request?

Include a clear description of the issue, the relevant category, and enough information for the support team to understand the request. Never include passwords, PINs, or one-time verification codes.
