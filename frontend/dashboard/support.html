Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Quick answers to common questions about the dashboard experience.

How do I view my accounts?

Open the Accounts section from the dashboard navigation to view your simulated account information and balances.

How can I review transactions?

Select Transactions from the dashboard navigation. You can use the available filters to review simulated activity.

Where can I manage my cards?

Open the Cards section to view your simulated cards and available card-management options.

How do I change my profile information?

Open Profile from the dashboard navigation. You can review the profile fields available for this simulation.

How do I sign out?

Use the Sign Out button at the bottom of the dashboard sidebar.

Is this a real banking service?

No. Global TrustFund is presented here as a digital banking simulation platform. The dashboard does not provide real banking, payment, deposit, lending, or financial services.
