Security

Access controlled at every level

Authentication verifies who you are. Authorization determines what you can access.

01

Authentication

Users must successfully authenticate through the configured identity system before reaching any protected banking resource.

02

Profile & Role

The backend determines each user’s trusted profile and assigned role. Client-side values are never used for authorization decisions.

03

Portal Separation

Customer, cashier, and management areas remain strictly separated according to server-enforced permissions.
