Responsible card use

Keep your card information protected.

Your card and account information should be treated as sensitive. Use your card carefully and review account activity regularly.

Protect your PIN Keep your PIN private and do not share it with others.
Protect card details Do not unnecessarily disclose card numbers or security information.
Review transactions Check your account activity and report unfamiliar transactions.
Contact support Contact the appropriate support channel when you need assistance.

Security is part of your banking experience.

Protect your login information and card credentials. GTF Banking will not ask you to disclose sensitive authentication information through an unverified channel.

Secure Access Use your official GTF Banking account access page.
Monitor Activity Review account and card activity regularly.
Report Concerns Contact support if you identify suspicious activity.
