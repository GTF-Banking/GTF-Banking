Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide general information about GTF Banking's approach to privacy and information protection. It should be read together with applicable account agreements, product terms, notices and other disclosures that apply to a particular banking service.

1. Overview

GTF Banking respects the importance of protecting information associated with its customers and visitors. We seek to handle information responsibly and use appropriate safeguards based on the nature of the information and the services involved.

By using a GTF Banking website, digital service or account, you may provide or generate information that is necessary to provide services, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements and improve the banking experience.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected may vary depending on the products and services you use and how you interact with GTF Banking.

Information you provide

This may include information provided when opening an account, submitting forms, contacting support or applying for a banking product.

Account information

Banking relationships may involve account, transaction, product and service information required to operate your account.

Technical information

Websites and digital services may collect technical information such as device, browser, connection and service-use data.

Communications

Information associated with customer support requests, service communications and other interactions may be retained where appropriate.

Information from other sources

Where permitted and appropriate, information may also be obtained from service providers, verification services, fraud-prevention systems or other lawful sources.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate banking, operational, security and customer service purposes, including:

  • Providing and administering requested banking products and services.
  • Processing and maintaining account and transaction records.
  • Authenticating customers and protecting accounts from unauthorized activity.
  • Responding to customer questions, requests and support inquiries.
  • Detecting, preventing and investigating fraud, abuse and other unauthorized activity.
  • Maintaining and improving websites, applications and banking services.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory and compliance obligations.
Important GTF Banking will not use account information for a purpose that is materially inconsistent with applicable notices, agreements or requirements without an appropriate basis for doing so.

4. Information Sharing

GTF Banking may share information where necessary to provide services, protect customers and the banking environment, operate systems, or meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Service providers

Certain functions may be performed by technology providers, payment providers, infrastructure providers, professional services firms or other authorized service providers.

Legal and regulatory requirements

Information may be disclosed when required or permitted by applicable law, regulation, court order or lawful governmental request.

Security and fraud prevention

Information may be shared with appropriate parties when reasonably necessary to detect, investigate or prevent fraud, security incidents or unauthorized activity.

Business operations

Information may be used or transferred as necessary to support legitimate corporate, operational or service-management activities, subject to applicable requirements.

5. Data Security

GTF Banking uses administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to help protect information against unauthorized access, use, alteration or disclosure.

Security controls may include authentication, access controls, monitoring, encryption or other protective measures appropriate to the systems and information involved.

Protect your credentials Never share your password, authentication code, PIN or other security credentials with another person. GTF Banking will not ask you to disclose sensitive authentication credentials through an unexpected message.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should also take reasonable steps to protect their devices, credentials and account information.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes, protect against fraud, enforce agreements and meet applicable legal, regulatory or operational requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it is used and applicable requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the applicable service, jurisdiction and legal requirements, customers may have choices or rights relating to their personal information.

  • Request access to certain information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Ask questions about how information is used.
  • Manage certain communications or preferences where available.
  • Request information about applicable privacy processes and controls.

Requests may be subject to identity verification, applicable law, contractual requirements and other legitimate limitations.

8. Cookies & Similar Technologies

GTF Banking websites or digital services may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, security, preferences, performance and service analytics.

Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect the operation or availability of some website functions.

9. Children's Privacy

GTF Banking services are not generally designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe information has been submitted improperly by a child, contact GTF Banking through the appropriate support channel so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Information Transfers

Depending on the banking service, infrastructure and service providers involved, information may be processed or stored in locations different from the location where you access the service.

Where applicable, GTF Banking will use appropriate measures and follow relevant legal requirements governing information transfers.

11. Third-Party Services

GTF Banking may provide links or integrations to third-party websites or services. Those third parties may have their own privacy policies and practices.

GTF Banking is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites that are outside its control. Review the applicable privacy notice before submitting information to a third party.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

GTF Banking may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in services, technology, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted through the appropriate GTF Banking channel and the effective or updated date may be revised.

13. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, contact GTF Banking through its customer-support channel.
