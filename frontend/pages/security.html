Protect your account

A few simple habits can make a big difference.

Keep your banking credentials private and pay attention to unexpected requests for account information.

  • Use a strong, unique password for your banking account.
  • Never share your password, PIN or authentication codes.
  • Review account activity regularly.
  • Keep your phone, computer and browser up to date.
  • Sign out when using a shared or public device.

Stay protected

Important security practices

Follow these practices whenever you use digital banking services.

01

Protect your login information

Do not give your password or authentication information to another person. Legitimate support personnel should not need your complete password.

02

Be careful with unexpected messages

Treat unexpected requests for banking details, passwords or verification codes with caution. Verify the request through an official channel.

03

Check website addresses

Before entering credentials, confirm that you are using the official GTF Banking website or application.

04

Monitor account activity

Review transactions and account activity. If something looks unfamiliar, contact the bank through an official support channel.

05

Secure your devices

Use device locks and keep operating systems, browsers and applications updated with available security updates.

06

Avoid sharing verification codes

Verification codes are intended for the account holder. Do not disclose them to people who ask for them unexpectedly.

Suspect unauthorized account activity?

If you believe your account or credentials may have been compromised, stop using the affected credentials and contact GTF Banking through an official support channel as soon as possible. Do not provide sensitive credentials through unexpected messages or unverified contacts.
