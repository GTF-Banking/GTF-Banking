SECURITY PRINCIPLES

Simple habits can make a difference

Protecting your banking information is a shared responsibility. Follow these basic practices whenever you use digital banking.

#

Use strong credentials

Use a strong, unique password for your banking account and avoid reusing it on other websites.

!

Watch for suspicious messages

Be cautious with unexpected messages, links or requests for account credentials.

✓

Protect your login

Never share your password, verification codes or other sensitive authentication information with another person.

→

Use trusted access

Access your account through the official Global TrustFund website or authorized application.

PROTECT YOUR ACCOUNT

Keep your credentials private

Your login credentials are personal. Do not give your password or security codes to someone who asks for them unexpectedly.

✓

Create a unique password

Avoid using the same password for your banking account and unrelated services.

✓

Keep verification codes private

Authentication codes should only be used by you for the intended sign-in process.

✓

Lock your device

Use your device's available screen-lock and security features.

✓

Security reminder

Global TrustFund will not ask you to disclose your password or authentication codes through an unexpected message.

Contact support →
STAY ALERT

Recognize suspicious activity

Fraud attempts can use convincing messages or websites. Pause and verify before taking action.

!

Unexpected requests

Treat unexpected requests for passwords, verification codes or sensitive information as suspicious.

→

Suspicious links

Avoid clicking unfamiliar links. Navigate directly to the official website instead.

✓

Verify first

If something seems unusual, stop and verify the request through an official support channel.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

Take action quickly

If you believe your account credentials may have been exposed or you notice suspicious activity, take appropriate protective action and contact support.

1

Stop interacting with the suspicious request

Do not provide additional credentials or verification information.

2

Secure your account

Sign in through an authorized channel and follow the available account security procedures.

3

Contact support

Report suspected unauthorized activity through the appropriate support channel.

BANK SAFELY

Stay informed. Stay protected.

Use secure banking practices every time you access your Global TrustFund account.
