1. Overview

Global TrustFund ("GTF", "we", "us", or "our") provides a digital banking simulation platform designed for demonstrations, learning, interface testing, and financial technology experiences.

This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information that may be processed when you interact with the platform and explains the general purposes for which that information may be used.

Important: Global TrustFund is not a real bank and does not provide real deposit-taking, banking, lending, investment, or payment services.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how the platform is configured, information associated with a user account may include:

  • Name and basic profile information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Country or location information provided by the user
  • Account preferences
  • Authentication and account-status information
  • Platform activity and transaction-simulation records
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the application

The exact information collected depends on the features enabled in the deployed application.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for legitimate platform purposes, including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts
  • Providing platform functionality
  • Supporting authentication and authorization
  • Maintaining application security
  • Troubleshooting technical issues
  • Improving the user experience
  • Demonstrating banking workflows
  • Preventing misuse of the platform

4. Security

Security controls may be used to protect account information and application infrastructure. Depending on the deployment, these controls may include authentication, authorization, access controls, encrypted connections, database security policies, logging, and monitoring.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also protect their credentials and avoid sharing authentication information with others.

Visit Security Center

5. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers or technical infrastructure providers when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve the platform.

The specific third-party services used by a deployment may vary. Users should review the applicable configuration and service documentation for the environment they are using.

We do not represent that the platform provides the same privacy framework or regulatory protections as a licensed financial institution.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The application may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar mechanisms to maintain application state, authentication state, preferences, or other necessary functionality.

Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies and locally stored information. Disabling certain technologies may affect platform functionality.

7. Your Choices

Depending on the application's configuration, users may be able to update profile information, manage account settings, sign out of active sessions, or request assistance through the support interface.

For questions concerning information associated with a platform account, contact the appropriate support channel.

Visit Support →

8. Children

The platform is not intended to request unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe information has been submitted by a child in a manner that should not have occurred, contact the platform administrator.

9. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform, features, infrastructure, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated policy may be published on this page together with a revised effective or update date.

10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Global TrustFund platform can be directed through the contact and support pages.
