Account protection

Protect your account with simple habits.

Security is shared between technology and user behavior. These practices can help improve account safety.

01

Use a strong password

Choose a unique password that is difficult for others to guess and avoid reusing important passwords across services.

02

Protect your credentials

Never intentionally share passwords, verification codes, or other authentication credentials with unknown people.

03

Review account activity

Regularly check account and transaction activity for information you do not recognize.

04

Use trusted devices

Keep your devices and browsers updated and avoid entering sensitive information on untrusted devices.

!
Stay alert

Be cautious with unexpected messages.

Suspicious messages may attempt to persuade you to reveal passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive information.

Security awareness

Stop and verify before responding.

Be cautious when a message creates urgency, requests confidential credentials, or directs you to an unfamiliar website.

  • ✓ Verify unexpected requests
  • ✓ Avoid sharing passwords
  • ✓ Check website addresses carefully
  • ✓ Contact support through trusted channels
