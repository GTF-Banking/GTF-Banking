Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Quick answers to some of the questions users commonly ask.

How do I create an account? +

Select Open Account from the navigation menu and complete the registration form. You may be required to confirm your email address before signing in.

I forgot my password. What should I do? +

Go to the password recovery page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

Why can't I sign in? +

Check that your email address and password are correct. If your account requires email confirmation, make sure you have completed that step. If the problem continues, contact support.

How do simulated transfers work? +

Transfers displayed within Global TrustFund are part of the platform's banking simulation. They do not represent movement of real money through a real financial institution.

Is Global TrustFund a real bank? +

Global TrustFund is presented as a digital banking simulation platform. It should not be treated as a licensed bank, deposit-taking institution, payment provider, or financial adviser.

Which browser should I use? +

Use a current version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, or another modern browser. Keeping your browser updated can also improve compatibility and security.
