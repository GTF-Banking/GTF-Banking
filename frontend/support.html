Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I create an account?

Select Open Account, complete the registration form and follow the verification instructions provided by the platform.

How do I sign in?

Select Sign In from the navigation menu and enter your registered email address and password.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password recovery option on the sign-in page to begin the account recovery process.

How do I contact support?

Complete the support form below with your contact information and a description of your issue.

How do I protect my account?

Use a strong unique password, protect your login information and avoid sharing authentication credentials with others.

Where can I find security information?

Visit the Global TrustFund Security Center for security-related information.
