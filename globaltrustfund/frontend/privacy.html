Privacy

How we handle information on the Global TrustFund platform.

Overview

Global TrustFund is a demonstration digital banking platform. This page describes general practices for data collected through the application.

Information we may collect

  • Account registration details (name, email, optional phone)
  • Authentication and session data
  • Support ticket content you submit
  • Technical logs needed for security and reliability

How we use information

  • To provide and improve the banking interface
  • To authenticate users and enforce access control
  • To respond to support requests
  • To maintain audit and security records where appropriate

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database services) strictly to operate the platform.

Your choices

You may request access or deletion of account data subject to legal and operational requirements. Contact support for assistance.

This privacy notice is provided for the demonstration platform and is not a substitute for a full legal policy required by a licensed financial institution.
