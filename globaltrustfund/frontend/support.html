Help Center

Answers to common questions and ways to get further assistance.

How do I open an account?

Use Open an Account to register with your name and email. After registration you can sign in to the customer dashboard.

I forgot my password

Go to Reset password and enter your email. If an account exists, reset instructions will be sent.

Where can I see my balances and transactions?

After signing in, open the Dashboard. Accounts and Transactions pages list balances and activity.

Is this a real bank?

No. Global TrustFund on this site is a demonstration platform. It is not a licensed bank and does not hold real customer deposits unless explicitly connected to a regulated institution.

How do I contact support?

Signed-in users can create tickets from the dashboard Support area. You can also use the Contact page for general inquiries.
